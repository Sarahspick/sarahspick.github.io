# Benchmark: what 40M+ view Shorts do (gym, sports, motivation, masculinity)

Sources studied
1. The five 40M+ references the channel owner uploaded (full frame and audio analysis):
   Bolt "Define Aura" (9.7 s), old gymnastics judging (13.0 s), high bar slip save (10.7 s),
   "He Never Gave Up" Bolt story (52.9 s), Korean AI hide and seek story (47.3 s).
2. YouTube Shorts search (sorted results, 30 queries, 1,024 Shorts): 61 Shorts above 40M views.
   Biggest themes: strength feats (200 kg shield carry 228M, World's Strongest Man 170M, Brian Shaw 118M),
   military precision and training (honor guard 139M, precision aim 107M, 100 hours with the Army 108M),
   aura / sigma edits (334M, 167M), respect and sportsmanship (154M, 151M, 135M), sprint and relay moments.
   (YouTube's bot check blocked watching these from the server, so only titles and view counts were used.)

What the winners share
- Length: 10 to 13 s for a single moment (they loop), about 50 s only for a full story.
- Hook in second one: the most striking frame first, plus a caption that opens a question
  ("Define Aura", "Old gymnastics judging was INSANE", "Teammate's reaction...").
- One short caption line (2 to 6 words) with 1 or 2 emoji (💀 😭 🥶 😳 🤯). It changes 2 to 4 times, only at story beats.
  White bold with a dark edge on video, or black bold on a white meme background.
- Callouts instead of effects: a plain red arrow, a hand-drawn red circle, an italic *action* label
  ("*BIG hop*", "*Hand slips*"), a punch-in zoom or slow motion on the key moment.
- Payoff: a reaction ("His Teammate:", "The Coach:"), a result (score, medal), or a twist caption ("The Disrespect 💀").
- Layouts: full-screen 9:16 crop, white meme background with the clip in the middle, or the clip on a blurred copy.
- Audio: a trending song carries most of them; one uses a text-to-speech hook line, the Korean one is fully narrated.
- No channel badge, no title bar, no glitch or shake effects, clean cuts only.

How the four new RageStyles shorts apply it
- Footage: US military public domain video (DVIDS), 1080p to 4K, natural sound only (clips with background music
  were rejected after an audio check). No YouTube or TikTok footage is reused.
- Music is left for the upload step (YouTube's licensed library), so no copyrighted song is baked in.
