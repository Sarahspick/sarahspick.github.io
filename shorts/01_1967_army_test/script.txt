In 1967, the U.S. Army had no gym. No machines. No protein shakes. Just this. A giant log, lifted overhead by the whole squad.
And every recruit had to pass a five event fitness test. Event one. A forty yard low crawl, against the clock.
Event two. The horizontal ladder. Your score? How many rungs you cross in one minute.
Event three. Dodge, run, and jump. Event four. Carry a grown man a hundred and fifty yards. Fast.
And event five. A full mile run. Five hundred points was a perfect score.
Pass it? Your reward was the confidence course. Obstacles named the Tough One, the Tarzan, and the Slide for Life.
So be honest. Would you pass the 1967 test?
